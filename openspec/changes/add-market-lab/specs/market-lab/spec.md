## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Agent-Based Market Model

The system SHALL model the market as a population of buyer agents and seller agents, where each buyer has a willingness-to-pay (WTP) for one unit of a good and each seller has a production cost for one unit. The system SHALL generate agent populations by sampling WTP and cost values from configurable probability distributions (uniform, normal) with user-adjustable mean and variance parameters. The system SHALL aggregate individual buyer preferences into a market demand curve (downward-sloping step function of quantity demanded at each price) and individual seller costs into a market supply curve (upward-sloping step function of quantity supplied at each price).

#### Scenario: Buyer agent with willingness-to-pay

- **WHEN** a buyer agent is created with WTP = $100
- **THEN** the buyer SHALL be willing to purchase one unit at any price ≤ $100 and unwilling to purchase at any price > $100

#### Scenario: Seller agent with production cost

- **WHEN** a seller agent is created with cost = $50
- **THEN** the seller SHALL be willing to sell one unit at any price ≥ $50 and unwilling to sell at any price < $50

#### Scenario: Demand curve from uniform WTP distribution

- **WHEN** 100 buyers are generated with WTP ~ Uniform(50, 150)
- **THEN** the demand curve SHALL be a descending step function where quantity demanded at price P equals the count of buyers with WTP ≥ P, and the curve SHALL be approximately linear from (0, 150) to (100, 50)

#### Scenario: Supply curve from uniform cost distribution

- **WHEN** 100 sellers are generated with cost ~ Uniform(50, 150)
- **THEN** the supply curve SHALL be an ascending step function where quantity supplied at price P equals the count of sellers with cost ≤ P, and the curve SHALL be approximately linear from (0, 50) to (100, 150)

#### Scenario: Agent population regeneration

- **WHEN** the user changes the distribution parameters (mean or variance) and triggers a reset
- **THEN** new agents SHALL be generated by resampling from the updated distribution, and the supply/demand curves SHALL update to reflect the new agent preferences

### Requirement: Price Discovery and Market Clearing

The system SHALL implement a double-auction clearing mechanism where the market price is determined as the unique price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied. The system SHALL compute the clearing price by sorting buyers in descending order of WTP, sorting sellers in ascending order of cost, finding the marginal index where the marginal buyer's WTP equals or exceeds the marginal seller's cost, and setting the clearing price as the midpoint of the marginal buyer's WTP and marginal seller's cost. The system SHALL match buyers and sellers for transaction execution: all buyers with WTP ≥ clearing price SHALL be paired with sellers with cost ≤ clearing price, up to the clearing quantity.

#### Scenario: Equilibrium price calculation

- **WHEN** 50 buyers have WTP = $100 and 50 sellers have cost = $50
- **THEN** the clearing price SHALL be ($100 + $50) / 2 = $75, and the clearing quantity SHALL be 50 units

#### Scenario: Transaction matching at clearing price

- **WHEN** the clearing price is $75 and clearing quantity is 50
- **THEN** exactly 50 buyers with WTP ≥ $75 SHALL be marked as traded, exactly 50 sellers with cost ≤ $75 SHALL be marked as traded, and all other agents SHALL remain untraded

#### Scenario: No trades possible when preferences do not overlap

- **WHEN** all buyers have WTP ≤ $40 and all sellers have cost ≥ $60
- **THEN** the clearing price SHALL be undefined, the clearing quantity SHALL be 0, and no agents SHALL be marked as traded

#### Scenario: Tie-breaking with identical preferences

- **WHEN** multiple buyers have identical WTP at the marginal price
- **THEN** the system SHALL randomly select which buyers trade to avoid systematic bias, ensuring fairness over multiple simulations

### Requirement: Step-Based Simulation Lifecycle

The system SHALL operate in discrete step-based mode using the DiscreteEngine simulation framework. Each step SHALL represent one market day, during which the following sequence occurs: (1) compute demand and supply curves from current agent populations, (2) apply active policy interventions to shift curves or constrain prices, (3) calculate the clearing price and quantity, (4) execute transactions and mark agents as traded or untraded, (5) compute welfare metrics (consumer surplus, producer surplus, deadweight loss, efficiency), (6) update price history and step counter, (7) return the new market state. The system SHALL provide step controls: next step button to advance one day, auto-step toggle to advance automatically at a configurable interval (200ms to 5000ms), reset button to restore initial state, and undo button to revert to the previous step.

#### Scenario: Single step execution

- **WHEN** the user clicks the "Next Step" button
- **THEN** the system SHALL execute one complete market day cycle (curve computation, clearing, transactions, welfare calculation), increment the step counter, update the canvas visualization, and await the next user action

#### Scenario: Auto-step at 1 second interval

- **WHEN** the user enables auto-step with interval = 1000ms
- **THEN** the system SHALL automatically execute one step every 1000ms until auto-step is disabled, a mission completes, or `canStep()` returns false

#### Scenario: Undo to previous step

- **WHEN** the user clicks the "Undo" button and step history contains at least one entry
- **THEN** the system SHALL restore the market state from the most recent history entry, decrement the step counter, remove the last entry from history, and re-render the canvas

#### Scenario: Reset to initial state

- **WHEN** the user clicks the "Reset" button
- **THEN** the system SHALL restore the state returned by `createInitialState()`, clear all step history, reset the step counter to 1, and re-render the canvas

#### Scenario: Step history tracking

- **WHEN** each step completes
- **THEN** the current market state (agent arrays, transaction records, telemetry values, intervention parameters) SHALL be stored in the step history stack up to a maximum depth of 50 entries

### Requirement: Policy Interventions

The system SHALL provide four types of policy interventions that users can apply to the market: price floor (minimum legal price), price ceiling (maximum legal price), per-unit tax on sellers, and per-unit subsidy to sellers. When a binding price floor is active (floor > free-market equilibrium price), the system SHALL set the market price equal to the floor and allow only the quantity where supply meets demand at that floor price, creating a surplus (quantity supplied > quantity demanded). When a binding price ceiling is active (ceiling < free-market equilibrium price), the system SHALL set the market price equal to the ceiling and allow only the quantity where supply meets demand at that ceiling price, creating a shortage (quantity demanded > quantity supplied). When a per-unit tax is imposed, the system SHALL shift the supply curve upward by the tax amount (increasing seller costs), resulting in a higher buyer price and lower quantity traded. When a per-unit subsidy is provided, the system SHALL shift the supply curve downward by the subsidy amount (decreasing seller costs), resulting in a lower buyer price and higher quantity traded.

#### Scenario: Binding price floor creates surplus

- **WHEN** the free-market equilibrium price is $75, and the user sets a price floor at $90
- **THEN** the market price SHALL be $90, the quantity demanded at $90 SHALL be less than the quantity supplied at $90, and the surplus (Q_supplied - Q_demanded) SHALL be a positive value displayed in telemetry

#### Scenario: Non-binding price floor has no effect

- **WHEN** the free-market equilibrium price is $75, and the user sets a price floor at $50
- **THEN** the market price SHALL remain $75, the clearing quantity SHALL be the free-market equilibrium quantity, and no surplus SHALL occur

#### Scenario: Binding price ceiling creates shortage

- **WHEN** the free-market equilibrium price is $75, and the user sets a price ceiling at $60
- **THEN** the market price SHALL be $60, the quantity demanded at $60 SHALL be greater than the quantity supplied at $60, and the shortage (Q_demanded - Q_supplied) SHALL be a positive value displayed in telemetry

#### Scenario: Per-unit tax reduces quantity traded

- **WHEN** a per-unit tax of $10 is imposed on sellers
- **THEN** the supply curve SHALL shift upward by $10 (each seller's effective cost increases by $10), the new equilibrium price paid by buyers SHALL be higher than the original price, the new equilibrium quantity traded SHALL be lower than the original quantity, and the difference between buyer price and seller net price SHALL equal $10

#### Scenario: Per-unit subsidy increases quantity traded

- **WHEN** a per-unit subsidy of $10 is provided to sellers
- **THEN** the supply curve SHALL shift downward by $10 (each seller's effective cost decreases by $10), the new equilibrium price paid by buyers SHALL be lower than the original price, and the new equilibrium quantity traded SHALL be higher than the original quantity

### Requirement: Welfare Metrics and Telemetry

The system SHALL compute and display welfare metrics after each step, including: consumer surplus (sum of (WTP - price) for all buyers who traded), producer surplus (sum of (price - cost) for all sellers who traded), total surplus (consumer surplus + producer surplus), deadweight loss (baseline free-market total surplus minus current total surplus due to interventions), market efficiency ((total surplus / max possible surplus) × 100), and surplus or shortage magnitude (quantity supplied minus quantity demanded at the intervention price). The system SHALL render telemetry values on the Canvas overlay in the top-right corner using IBM Plex Mono font at 14px, with labels in Economics accent color #2a9d8f and values in white. The system SHALL format values as follows: prices and surplus values as dollars with two decimal places, efficiency as percentage with one decimal place, and surplus/shortage as signed integers.

#### Scenario: Consumer surplus calculation at equilibrium

- **WHEN** 100 buyers with WTP ~ Uniform(50, 150) trade at equilibrium price $100
- **THEN** consumer surplus SHALL equal the sum of (WTP - $100) for the 50 buyers who traded (those with WTP ≥ $100), which for a uniform distribution is approximately (150 - 100) × 50 / 2 = $1250

#### Scenario: Producer surplus calculation at equilibrium

- **WHEN** 100 sellers with cost ~ Uniform(50, 150) trade at equilibrium price $100
- **THEN** producer surplus SHALL equal the sum of ($100 - cost) for the 50 sellers who traded (those with cost ≤ $100), which for a uniform distribution is approximately (100 - 50) × 50 / 2 = $1250

#### Scenario: Total surplus at free-market equilibrium

- **WHEN** the market operates at free-market equilibrium with no interventions
- **THEN** total surplus SHALL equal consumer surplus plus producer surplus, and deadweight loss SHALL be zero

#### Scenario: Deadweight loss from binding price floor

- **WHEN** a binding price floor reduces the quantity traded from the free-market equilibrium quantity
- **THEN** deadweight loss SHALL be the difference between the baseline total surplus (at free-market equilibrium) and the current total surplus (at the floor price), representing the welfare lost from prevented trades

#### Scenario: Market efficiency below 100% with intervention

- **WHEN** a policy intervention (floor, ceiling, or tax) is active and reduces total surplus
- **THEN** market efficiency SHALL be less than 100%, calculated as (current total surplus / baseline total surplus) × 100

#### Scenario: Telemetry display format

- **WHEN** the clearing price is $87.53, consumer surplus is $1234.56, and market efficiency is 87.3%
- **THEN** the telemetry SHALL display "Price: $87.53", "Consumer Surplus: $1234.56", and "Efficiency: 87.3%" using IBM Plex Mono font with Economics accent color labels

### Requirement: Mission Find Equilibrium

The system SHALL provide a "Find Equilibrium" mission that challenges the user to adjust the number of buyers and sellers until the market clears with minimal imbalance. The mission SHALL evaluate success per-step by checking that no policy interventions are active (price floor, ceiling, tax, subsidy all disabled or non-binding), the absolute difference between quantity supplied and quantity demanded is less than 5% of the maximum of the two, and total surplus is at least 95% of the theoretical maximum possible surplus. The mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state when all criteria are met and display a congratulatory message. There SHALL be no failure state for this mission; users experiment freely until equilibrium is discovered.

#### Scenario: Successful equilibrium discovery

- **WHEN** the user sets 100 buyers and 100 sellers with matching WTP and cost distributions, no interventions are active, and a step is executed
- **THEN** the market SHALL clear with |Q_supplied - Q_demanded| < 5% and total surplus ≥ 95% of maximum, and the mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state with message "Market cleared! Supply met demand."

#### Scenario: Imbalanced supply prevents success

- **WHEN** the user sets 150 sellers and 50 buyers, and a step is executed
- **THEN** the quantity supplied SHALL significantly exceed quantity demanded (surplus > 5%), and the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state, prompting the user to adjust agent counts

#### Scenario: Active intervention prevents success

- **WHEN** the user has balanced buyer/seller counts but a binding price floor is active
- **THEN** the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state because an intervention is distorting the market, and a hint SHALL suggest removing interventions to achieve free-market equilibrium

### Requirement: Mission Price Floor Disaster

The system SHALL provide a "Price Floor Disaster" mission that demonstrates the unintended consequences of binding minimum price regulations. The mission SHALL evaluate success per-step by checking that a price floor is set above the free-market equilibrium price (binding floor), the resulting surplus (quantity supplied minus quantity demanded at the floor price) is at least 20% of the quantity supplied, and deadweight loss is greater than 10% of the baseline free-market total surplus. The mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state when all criteria are met and display a message emphasizing the surplus of unsold units and stranded sellers.

#### Scenario: Successful demonstration of price floor surplus

- **WHEN** the free-market equilibrium is at $75 with 50 units traded, the user sets a price floor at $100, and a step is executed
- **THEN** the market price SHALL be $100, quantity supplied SHALL exceed quantity demanded, the surplus SHALL be at least 20% of quantity supplied, deadweight loss SHALL exceed 10% of baseline surplus, and the mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state with message "Surplus created — some sellers cannot sell."

#### Scenario: Non-binding floor does not create surplus

- **WHEN** the user sets a price floor at $50 when equilibrium is $75
- **THEN** the floor SHALL be non-binding (market price remains $75), no surplus SHALL be created, and the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state, instructing the user to set a higher floor

#### Scenario: Deadweight loss threshold not met

- **WHEN** the user sets a price floor slightly above equilibrium, creating only a small surplus
- **THEN** deadweight loss may be less than 10% of baseline surplus, and the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state until the floor is set high enough to demonstrate significant welfare loss

### Requirement: Mission The Invisible Hand

The system SHALL provide a "The Invisible Hand" mission that demonstrates how markets self-organize to maximize welfare when interventions are removed. The mission SHALL begin with active policy distortions (price floor, ceiling, or tax) that reduce market efficiency below 70%. The mission SHALL evaluate success by detecting when the user removes all interventions (sets floor to non-binding, ceiling to non-binding, tax and subsidy to zero), allows the market to run for at least 5 auto-steps, and observes market efficiency rise to 95% or higher. The mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state when all criteria are met and display a message referencing Adam Smith's invisible hand principle.

#### Scenario: Efficiency improvement after removing interventions

- **WHEN** the mission starts with a binding price floor that reduces efficiency to 65%, the user sets the floor to zero, enables auto-step, and 5 steps execute
- **THEN** the market SHALL return to free-market equilibrium, efficiency SHALL rise to at least 95%, and the mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state with message "The invisible hand at work — welfare maximized without central control."

#### Scenario: Interventions remain active prevents success

- **WHEN** the user reduces but does not fully remove interventions (e.g., lowers price floor but it remains binding)
- **THEN** efficiency SHALL remain below 95%, and the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state, instructing the user to fully remove all controls

#### Scenario: Auto-step requirement enforced

- **WHEN** the user removes interventions but manually steps only 3 times
- **THEN** the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state because the 5-step observation period has not completed, ensuring users observe the convergence process

### Requirement: Mission Market Crash

The system SHALL provide a "Market Crash" mission that demonstrates speculative bubble dynamics and market fragility. The system SHALL introduce a "speculation" parameter that temporarily inflates all buyer WTP values by a configurable multiplier (default 1.5×). The mission SHALL evaluate success by detecting a bubble phase (speculation active, market price >50% above baseline equilibrium, quantity traded increases) followed by a crash phase (speculation removed, market price drops >40% in a single step, quantity traded falls >30% in a single step). The mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state when both bubble and crash thresholds are met and display a message explaining the speculative collapse.

#### Scenario: Successful bubble and crash cycle

- **WHEN** the baseline equilibrium is $75, the user enables speculation at 1.5× multiplier, price rises to $120 (60% above baseline), then the user disables speculation, and the next step executes
- **THEN** buyer WTP values SHALL revert to original distribution, price SHALL drop to near $75 (40%+ drop from $120), quantity traded SHALL fall significantly (>30%), and the mission SHALL transition to SUCCESS state with message "Market crashed — speculative bubble burst."

#### Scenario: Bubble phase not established

- **WHEN** the user enables speculation but the price increase is only 30% above baseline (below the 50% threshold)
- **THEN** the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state, instructing the user to increase the speculation multiplier or adjust agent distributions to create a larger bubble

#### Scenario: Crash magnitude insufficient

- **WHEN** the user disables speculation but the price drop is only 25% (below the 40% threshold)
- **THEN** the mission SHALL remain in ACTIVE state because the crash was not dramatic enough to demonstrate fragility, prompting the user to create more extreme bubble conditions before triggering the crash

### Requirement: Sandbox Mode

The system SHALL provide a sandbox mode where all mission constraints are disabled, allowing free experimentation with market parameters. In sandbox mode, the system SHALL unlock all adjustable parameters: number of buyers (1-200), number of sellers (1-200), WTP distribution parameters (mean, variance, shape: uniform or normal), cost distribution parameters (mean, variance, shape), price floor (0-500), price ceiling (0-500), per-unit tax (0-100), per-unit subsidy (0-100), and speculation multiplier (1.0-3.0). The system SHALL disable all mission condition checks and allow unlimited step advancement. The system SHALL provide a reset button to restore initial conditions without exiting sandbox mode.

#### Scenario: All parameters unlocked in sandbox

- **WHEN** the user enters sandbox mode
- **THEN** all parameter sliders and inputs SHALL be enabled, allowing adjustment of buyer/seller counts, distributions, and intervention parameters without restriction

#### Scenario: No mission constraints in sandbox

- **WHEN** the user creates an extreme distortion (e.g., price floor at $500 with no buyers able to purchase)
- **THEN** the system SHALL execute steps normally, display resulting telemetry (zero trades, zero surplus), and NOT transition to any mission state or display mission messages

#### Scenario: Reset in sandbox clears history

- **WHEN** the user steps forward 20 times in sandbox mode, then clicks reset
- **THEN** the market state SHALL revert to the initial conditions defined by `createInitialState()`, the step counter SHALL reset to 1, and the step history SHALL be cleared

### Requirement: Supply and Demand Curve Visualization

The system SHALL render supply and demand curves on a 2D Canvas chart with price on the Y-axis and quantity on the X-axis. The demand curve SHALL be a descending step function (or optionally smoothed curve) connecting buyer WTP values sorted in descending order, rendered in blue (#3a86ff). The supply curve SHALL be an ascending step function (or optionally smoothed curve) connecting seller cost values sorted in ascending order, rendered in orange (#fb8500). The system SHALL mark the equilibrium point with a large filled circle at the intersection of the two curves and draw horizontal and vertical dashed lines from the equilibrium to the axes, showing equilibrium price and quantity. The system SHALL shade consumer surplus (area below demand curve and above equilibrium price) in translucent blue (alpha 0.3) and producer surplus (area above supply curve and below equilibrium price) in translucent green (#06d6a0, alpha 0.3). When policy interventions are active and create deadweight loss, the system SHALL shade the deadweight loss region (triangular area between curves and intervention price) in translucent red (#ef476f, alpha 0.3).

#### Scenario: Free-market equilibrium visualization

- **WHEN** 100 buyers with WTP ~ Uniform(50, 150) and 100 sellers with cost ~ Uniform(50, 150) reach equilibrium at price $100 and quantity 50
- **THEN** the Canvas SHALL display a descending blue demand curve from (0, 150) to (100, 50), an ascending orange supply curve from (0, 50) to (100, 150), an equilibrium point marked at (50, 100), and shaded consumer and producer surplus regions

#### Scenario: Binding price floor visualization with surplus

- **WHEN** a binding price floor is set at $110, creating a surplus where quantity supplied > quantity demanded
- **THEN** the Canvas SHALL display a thick horizontal red line at price $110, shaded consumer and producer surplus regions at the floor price (smaller than free-market), and a shaded red deadweight loss triangle representing the welfare lost from prevented trades

#### Scenario: Dynamic axis scaling

- **WHEN** the user changes the WTP distribution mean from 100 to 200
- **THEN** the Y-axis SHALL rescale to accommodate the new price range (0 to 220 with 10% padding), and the demand curve SHALL shift upward accordingly

#### Scenario: Policy marker rendering

- **WHEN** a per-unit tax of $20 is applied
- **THEN** the original supply curve SHALL be rendered as a faint dotted line, the new tax-shifted supply curve SHALL be rendered as a solid orange line shifted upward by $20, and an arrow SHALL indicate the direction and magnitude of the shift

### Requirement: Price History Visualization

The system SHALL render a secondary line chart below the main supply/demand chart, showing market price over time. The X-axis SHALL represent step index, and the Y-axis SHALL represent price. The system SHALL display the price for the last 20 steps as a continuous line graph rendered in Economics accent color #2a9d8f. When the step count exceeds 20, the system SHALL implement a rolling window, removing the oldest data points as new steps are added. The system SHALL mark intervention changes on the price history chart with vertical dotted lines labeled with the change (e.g., "Floor set to $90").

#### Scenario: Price history updates after each step

- **WHEN** the user steps forward 5 times with equilibrium price $75
- **THEN** the price history chart SHALL display 5 data points, all at price $75, connected by a line

#### Scenario: Rolling window truncates old data

- **WHEN** the user steps forward 25 times
- **THEN** the price history chart SHALL display only the most recent 20 steps, with the earliest 5 steps removed from the chart

#### Scenario: Intervention markers on price history

- **WHEN** the user sets a price floor at step 10, changing the price from $75 to $90
- **THEN** a vertical dotted line SHALL appear at step 10 on the price history chart, labeled "Floor: $90", and the price line SHALL jump from $75 to $90 at that step

### Requirement: Reference Panel

The system SHALL provide a reference panel as a React component that displays educational content on economic concepts relevant to the simulation. The panel SHALL include collapsible sections for: (1) Supply and Demand curves with explanations of slopes and agent-based foundations, (2) Equilibrium definition and condition Q_d(P*) = Q_s(P*), (3) Consumer and Producer Surplus definitions and area formulas, (4) Price Elasticity definition and implications for tax incidence, (5) Market Failures including price floors, ceilings, taxes, and subsidies with deadweight loss explanations, and (6) Invisible Hand principle with Adam Smith quote and welfare maximization explanation. The panel SHALL implement contextual highlighting: the section most relevant to the current mission SHALL be highlighted in Economics accent color #2a9d8f. The panel SHALL use brand fonts: Instrument Serif for headings, DM Sans for body text, and IBM Plex Mono for equations.

#### Scenario: Reference panel displays economic concepts

- **WHEN** the user opens the reference panel
- **THEN** all six sections SHALL be visible, each with a heading, explanatory text, and optional diagrams or equations

#### Scenario: Collapsible sections

- **WHEN** the user clicks on a section heading (e.g., "Price Elasticity")
- **THEN** the section SHALL expand to show full content if collapsed, or collapse to show only the heading if expanded, allowing users to focus on relevant concepts

#### Scenario: Contextual highlighting during mission

- **WHEN** the "Price Floor Disaster" mission is active
- **THEN** the "Market Failures" section SHALL be highlighted in Economics accent color, drawing user attention to the relevant educational content

#### Scenario: Mobile responsive reference panel

- **WHEN** the simulation is loaded on a mobile device
- **THEN** the reference panel SHALL appear as a bottom sheet that swipes up on tap, overlaying the canvas, and swipes down to dismiss

### Requirement: Touch and Mobile Controls

The system SHALL provide touch-optimized controls on devices with touch capability. Parameter sliders SHALL have a minimum touch target height of 44px to meet WCAG accessibility guidelines. Step control buttons (next, auto-step, reset, undo) SHALL be enlarged to 48px×48px for easy tapping. The reference panel SHALL collapse to a bottom sheet interface that opens with an upward swipe gesture. Auto-step interval adjustment SHALL use a large slider with visible thumb control. The system SHALL detect touch capability using `'ontouchstart' in window` or `navigator.maxTouchPoints > 0` and apply touch-optimized UI only on touch-enabled devices.

#### Scenario: Enlarged touch targets on mobile

- **WHEN** the simulation is loaded on a touchscreen device
- **THEN** all parameter sliders SHALL have height ≥ 44px, and all step control buttons SHALL be 48px×48px or larger

#### Scenario: Bottom sheet reference panel on touch devices

- **WHEN** the user taps the reference panel toggle on a touchscreen device
- **THEN** the reference panel SHALL slide up from the bottom of the screen as an overlay, covering the lower portion of the canvas, and SHALL slide down when the user taps outside or swipes down

#### Scenario: Touch gesture for auto-step interval

- **WHEN** the user drags the auto-step interval slider on a touchscreen
- **THEN** the slider thumb SHALL follow the touch point smoothly, and the interval value SHALL update in real-time, with the new interval taking effect immediately if auto-step is active
